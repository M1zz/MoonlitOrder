🎲What is Moonlit Arcade?

Moonlit Arcade is a collection of social deduction and party games that connect nearby iPhones directly, with no internet required. As long as everyone is on the same Wi-Fi or within Bluetooth range, you can create and join a room right away, no servers involved.

🌙 Moonlit Order
5–15 players · Unmask the Shadows hiding among you
🎭 Liar Game
3–15 players · Find the one who doesn’t know the word
🔥 Moonless Night
3–15 players · One night of deduction with Goblins in your midst
🖌️ Moonlit Studio
3–15 players · One player draws, everyone else guesses

▶️How to Play

  • Enter a nickname and choose a game.
  • One person taps “Create a Room (Host)”, and everyone else enters the room with “Join a Game”.
  • Devices on the same Wi-Fi or close by (Bluetooth) find each other automatically. No internet connection is needed.
  • The first time you connect, you need to allow local network access so the app can find nearby devices.
  • Every game has “How to Play (Practice with Bots)”, where you can learn the rules step by step alongside bots.

🛠️Frequently Asked Questions

I can’t see any rooms nearby.
Make sure the host and the other players are on the same Wi-Fi or close to one another. Moonlit Arcade must be turned on in Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network. Keeping both Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on gives you the most reliable connection.
I got disconnected during a game.
Just reopen the app. It will reconnect automatically to the same room and put you back in the round you were playing. If it doesn’t reconnect within a certain amount of time, please join again from the room list.
What’s the minimum number of players?
Moonlit Order needs at least 5 players, and the other games can start with 3. Up to 15 people can play together.
Do I need internet access or an account?
No. You play offline, with no sign-up, login, or internet connection. Game data is never sent to a server.
